# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability

Email [security@longsurf.ai](mailto:security@longsurf.ai) to report a suspected
security vulnerability privately.

Include the affected Reagent version or commit, your operating system, steps
to reproduce the issue, and the potential impact. Share a minimal reproduction
when possible, and remove credentials and personal data from logs or screenshots.

Please keep vulnerability details out of public issues, pull requests, and
community channels while we investigate and coordinate disclosure with you.

For bugs without a security impact, use
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/longsurf-ai/reagent/issues).
